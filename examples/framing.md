# Plan a revision of a materials research paper

## Request

Read the draft and lab record below. Explain why the argument is hard to follow and propose a new outline. Keep this at the planning stage.

## Draft excerpt

**P1.** Surface coatings can extend the service life of metal components in marine environments. Electrochemical impedance spectroscopy is widely used to study corrosion.

**P2.** The coated coupons had a higher median low-frequency impedance after 24 h than the untreated coupons. This demonstrates the coating's suitability for long-term marine service.

**P3.** The specimens were exposed to one chloride concentration at 25 °C. Six independently prepared coupons were tested per group. Measurements were made after 24 h.

**P4.** Coating studies have used different immersion durations. The technique used here also offers a rapid screening measurement.

## Lab record

All measurements were made in one laboratory. The coating formulation was tested against untreated controls under one immersion condition. The observed median low-frequency impedance was 1.8 times as high in the coated group. No uncertainty estimate, long-term exposure, or field result was reported. No literature search results are supplied.
