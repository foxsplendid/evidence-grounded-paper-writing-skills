# Synthesize evidence on building energy retrofits

## Request

Plan a short narrative review section on the conditions under which advanced controls reduce electricity use in retrofitted office buildings. Group the studies around a comparison and flag any claims that need full-text verification.

## Source cards

These fictional cards reproduce all the source material available for this exercise.

- **Study A, methods and results excerpts.** In six occupied offices already fitted with efficient chillers, advanced controls were added at the start of summer. Meter readings were compared with the same offices in the previous summer after weather adjustment. Total cooling electricity use declined by 8%. Occupancy was not measured.
- **Study B, methods and results excerpts.** Eight offices using the same chiller type were assigned to advanced controls or existing schedules. Room occupancy was measured. During a four-week shoulder-season trial, cooling electricity use was 2% lower with advanced controls, with a 95% confidence interval from 6% lower to 2% higher.
- **Study C, abstract only.** A simulation estimates up to 20% cooling electricity savings for a combined chiller-and-controls retrofit in a hot climate. The abstract does not give the calibration protocol, the comparator configuration, or savings for controls alone.
- **Study D, methods and results excerpts.** In the six offices from Study A, the same electricity records were reanalysed with an alternative weather adjustment. Estimated summer cooling electricity use was 7% lower.
