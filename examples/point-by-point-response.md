# Draft a point-by-point response for a fatigue study

## Request

Draft a brief point-by-point response based on the revised material. Use section or figure names to locate changes. The typeset pages are unavailable.

## Reviewer comments

1. The scope of the fatigue conclusion is too broad. Please specify test conditions in the Results and figure caption.
2. Add high-humidity experiments, or explain how the lack of high-humidity data affects the conclusion.
3. Please replace Fig. 2 with a table listing sample counts by group and the uncertainty interval. Readers should be able to retrieve the values without estimating from the plot.

## Submitted baseline

- **Results, Fatigue tests.** “The treatment doubled fatigue life in service.”
- **Figure 2.** Bar chart with group medians of 20,000 and 10,000 cycles. Caption “Fatigue life after treatment.”
- **Discussion, Study limitations.** No explicit humidity limitation.

## Revised manuscript excerpts and author decisions

- **Results, Fatigue tests.** “At 23 °C and 40% relative humidity, the median fatigue life was 20,000 cycles for treated specimens and 10,000 cycles for controls (12 specimens per group).”
- **Figure 2.** The chart is retained. Panel B now lists sample counts and group medians. Caption “Fatigue life at 23 °C and 40% relative humidity. Panel B gives the sample count and median fatigue life for each group.”
- **Discussion, Study limitations.** “The tests covered one humidity level. The response to higher humidity remains untested.”
- No high-humidity experiments were conducted. No interval was calculated by the authors. The team retained the figure so readers can inspect the individual data points added in panel A.
